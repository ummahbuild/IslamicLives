In the Qur’an

Musa asks that his brother Harun be appointed to strengthen him in his task.

The account continues

The Qur’an describes Harun’s appointment as a prophet as a gift of divine mercy to Musa.

A place named in the account

Egypt is explicitly named in the instruction addressed to Musa and his brother. This is an associated region, not a verified birthplace.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
