# Prophet profile fact-check

Checked: 2026-09-08

## Scope and result

All 25 profiles in the traditionally taught list were checked for internal source alignment: each displayed biographical claim is tied to its cited Qur’an passage or labelled hadith report, undated Qur’anic lives remain undated, and uncertain locations remain unplotted. Every profile now also has a curated key-Qur’an-passage index and an explicit Sunnah coverage status.

This is a source-alignment check, not a declaration that every interpretation is uncontested or that every Qur’an narrative reference, tafsir work, or hadith collection has been exhausted. Qualified scholarly review remains a separate release gate.

## Important boundaries preserved

- The Qur’an does not supply calendar dates for the early prophet accounts; no BCE dates were added.
- The son in the sacrifice passage at 37:101–107 is not named in those verses, so that passage is not silently presented as a direct named reference under Ismail.
- Dhul-Kifl remains on the traditional list, with the disagreement about whether the cited verses establish prophethood disclosed.
- The key-passage lists are narrative reading guides. They are broader than the Quranic Arabic Corpus name-token index but are not exhaustive tafsir indexes.
- Hadith pages are identified as transmitted religious reports, not independent archaeological or chronological evidence.

## Current Sunnah coverage

Seventeen profiles have a verified reference in the current Sahih al-Bukhari-first set. Eight have an explicit research gap. “Research gap” does not mean no report exists; it means no prophet-specific report has yet passed this project’s present verification boundary.
