ابن بطوطة

Ibn Battuta

A traveller’s account

Journeys from 1325 · Tangier and Afro-Eurasia

Leaving home

Ibn Battuta left Tangier in 1325 to undertake the pilgrimage to Mecca. His travels continued far beyond the initial pilgrimage.

Remembering the journey

His recollections became the Rihla, dictated after his return. It is an important account of connected Muslim societies, but a travel narrative requires critical reading rather than treating every passage as an independently confirmed visit.

Dates and routes in the Rihla are debated. The map marks Tangier, his point of departure, not every claimed destination.
