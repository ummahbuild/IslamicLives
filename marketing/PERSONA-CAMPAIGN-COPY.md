## 7. Qualified scholars — “Scoped review, not a rubber stamp”
**Email subject:** Invitation: bounded scholarly review of IslamicLives biographies

Assalamu alaikum,

We're developing IslamicLives, a public educational project from the ummah.build community. Its working edition includes 81 biographical profiles and a historical atlas. The editorial approach distinguishes Qur'anic passages, transmitted hadith, historical materials and uncertainty.

We're seeking qualified reviewers for precisely defined claim sets—not broad endorsement. Most profiles do not yet have documented qualified-scholar review, and we want to improve that responsibly.
