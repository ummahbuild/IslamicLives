Many Muslim political worlds

Rule & religious institutions

Ottoman expansion linked Anatolia, southeastern Europe and Arab lands. Safavid rulers established Shi‘i state patronage in Iran, while Mughal rule grew in South Asia. Their societies remained religiously diverse.

Map markers, routes, and turquoise extents are illustrative context—not borders, ownership, exact paths, or conversion percentages.
