## Before representing the *entire* repository as fully MIT
Maintainers should verify authority to license existing code and any employer/contractor contributions; review content/data provenance and third-party source terms; ensure contributor agreement on licensing for future changes; and record rights review in [issue #1](https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives/issues/1). Do not claim that adding this file cures earlier third-party restrictions.

## Contribution expectations
Contributors should submit only work they are authorized to contribute and identify third-party assets or text. Contribution licensing under MIT should be explained and confirmed in CONTRIBUTING.md after a maintainer rights review; no contributor can grant rights they do not own.
