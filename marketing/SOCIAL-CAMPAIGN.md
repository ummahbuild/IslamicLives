## Carousel: Introducing IslamicLives (7 cards)
1. **IslamicLives** — Discover the lives. Follow the evidence. (ummah.build community project)
2. **One life, many dimensions** — When → Where → Life → Story.
3. **81 profiles today** — Prophets, Companions, scholars, scientists, travelers and other historical figures.
4. **Explore the atlas** — Discover illustrative historical geography across 16 chapters.
5. **Go to the sources** — Qur'an, hadith and historical accounts are distinguished.
6. **Questions belong in learning** — Uncertain details and research limits remain visible.
7. **Help build it** — Browse the public repository, submit a correction or contribute.

Caption: A member of our ummah.build community helped bring IslamicLives to life. Here's a look at the learning journey we're building, and how you can help improve it. https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives

## Carousel: What makes history reliable? (5 cards)
1. **A compelling story isn't necessarily a verified story.**
2. **Name the source:** Qur'anic passage, hadith report, historical chronicle or contemporary academic research?
3. **Locate the claim:** exact verse range, report number, text and context.
4. **State disagreement:** alternative dates and interpretations should remain visible.
5. **Leave room for correction:** building trustworthy learning tools requires humility. Help improve IslamicLives on GitHub.

## 45-second community spotlight
Hook: “One thing we're proud of at ummah.build? Seeing members turn learning ideas into things people can build on.”
Middle: Show a real approved clip or screenshots of profile browsing, atlas and source links.
Point: “IslamicLives makes history more navigable without pretending every historical question is settled.”
Close: “The repository is public. If you research, design, code or teach, we'd love careful feedback.”
Attribution: Obtain the contributor's preferred credit before publishing their name or portrait.

## 60-second educator explainer
Ask: “How do we help students tell a primary source from a later retelling?”
Demonstrate: Choose a verified example profile; show evidence label; click source; note uncertainty; discuss what remains unknown.
Close: “The tool is a starting point, not a replacement for qualified teachers or critical source work.”

## Community moderation and response bank
**Is this exhaustive?** No, it is a growing collection.
**Were all profiles scholar-reviewed?** No; we are inviting qualified, scoped review.
**Is the map historically exact?** No; illustrative geography is not exact border or route data.
**Can I reuse this code?** The source is public, but licensing hasn't been declared; await the maintainer's explicit license.
**I found a factual issue.** Thank you—please open a bounded issue with the precise claim and a traceable source.

## Measurement
Track repository visits, README-to-CONTRIBUTING clicks (where measurable), issues opened, accepted source fixes, qualified reviewer volunteers, PRs merged, source corrections and accessible-use defects. Do not confuse raw reach with historical accuracy.
