# Requested additions source-alignment review

Reviewed: 2026-09-08

## Scope

This covers the 18 profiles added in the atlas/profile expansion: five Qur’anic profiles, ten Companion profiles, and three academic biographies. It checks whether displayed wording stays within cited sources. It is not an exhaustive corpus review, isnad study, or qualified-scholar approval.

## Academic biographies

- **Al-Ghazali:** [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/al-Ghazali). Birth-year alternatives remain visible rather than becoming false precision.
- **Ibn Rushd:** [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ibn-rushd/). This supports 1126–1198, Cordoba, his judicial family and service, medicine, religious sciences, and Aristotelian commentaries. It also says important parts of his education and late disgrace remain unclear.
- **Ibn Khaldun:** [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ibn-Khaldun). The profile avoids treating “founder of sociology” as a precise description of his medieval project.

## Result

No unsupported precise dates or locations were added to the five Qur’anic profiles. Companion profiles remain report-centred rather than claiming complete biography. Scholar profiles retain their major uncertainty notes.
