عائشة بنت أبي بكر

Aisha bint Abi Bakr

A transmitter of knowledge

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

Preserving an account

Aisha is the named transmitter of the account of the beginning of revelation in Sahih al-Bukhari. Her narration preserves the story of Muhammad’s return to Khadijah.

A source of understanding

A report from Abu Musa describes the Companions consulting Aisha when they needed help understanding a hadith. It bears witness to her place in the transmission of religious knowledge.

These are reports within Sunni hadith collections. They are not a comprehensive account of her life.
