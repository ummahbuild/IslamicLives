Around two billion people

Demography & migration

Pew estimates about two billion Muslims worldwide in 2020. Its research identifies a younger population and higher fertility as important drivers of growth. Migration also changes where communities live.

Map markers, routes, and turquoise extents are illustrative context—not borders, ownership, exact paths, or conversion percentages.
