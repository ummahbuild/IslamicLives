# IslamicLives — virtual community launch session

**Working title:** IslamicLives: Building Beneficial Knowledge Together
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes; date/time to be approved.
**Status:** event proposal, not scheduled.

## Positioning
A transparent project showcase and co-building invitation, not a scholarly certification event or fully launched app demonstration.

## Agenda
00–05 Welcome and ummah.build mission; honor the member's initiative (approved attribution only).
05–15 Why IslamicLives? The learning problem and When → Where → Life → Story.
15–25 Repository walkthrough: search, source-linked profiles, atlas—only verified live features shown.
25–35 Evidence and humility: what is sourced, what is uncertain, what needs expert review.
35–45 How to contribute: one bounded source correction, issue or usability report.
45–60 Q&A, next steps and community feedback.

## Moderator introduction
Assalamu alaikum and welcome. We're gathering to celebrate a community-born project called IslamicLives and explore what it could become when people build with intention and care. Today's session is a project walkthrough and an invitation to contribute—not a claim that every historical account is complete or scholar-reviewed.

## Event listing
**IslamicLives: Discover the Lives. Follow the Evidence.**

Join the ummah.build community for a look at IslamicLives, a growing educational project initiated with help from one of our members. Explore source-linked profiles, an illustrative historical atlas, and the engineering and editorial decisions behind the experience. We'll discuss opportunities for contributors to improve research, accessibility, design and mobile reliability.

For developers, educators, researchers and curious learners. The project is still under active development. Event timing and demo URL will be announced when approved.

Repository: https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives
