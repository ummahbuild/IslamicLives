Uzayr

A name in a theological passage

Date not established · Location not established

In the source

Uzayr is named in a Qur’anic passage rejecting claims of divine sonship.

The verse is a theological statement, not a full biography or a description of what all Jewish people in every period believe. His prophetic status is not asserted here.
