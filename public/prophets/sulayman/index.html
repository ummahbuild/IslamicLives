سليمان

Sulayman

Understanding and judgement

Date not established · Location not established

In the Qur’an

Sulayman appears with Dawud in the account of a judgement concerning a field.

The account continues

Allah grants Sulayman understanding of the case, while affirming the judgement and knowledge bestowed on both men.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: Sahih al-Bukhari transmits a report about Sulayman and the importance of saying “if Allah wills.”
