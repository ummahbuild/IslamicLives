Hadith

Preparing provisions

Asma reports preparing provisions for Muhammad and Abu Bakr at the time of migration and dividing her waistband to tie their containers.

A name from the report

The same report explains the title associated with the two waistbands. This profile does not build a full life from that single episode.

Her exact birth date is not established here; later biographical detail requires a broader source review.
