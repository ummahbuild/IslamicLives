Prophets · sourced profile

عيسى

Isa

A messenger to the Children of Israel

Date not established · The Children of Israel; no birthplace asserted

In the Qur’an

In the Qur’anic account, Isa identifies himself as a servant of Allah who has received scripture and been made a prophet.

The account continues

He is sent to the Children of Israel with signs, including healing, explicitly described as occurring by Allah’s permission.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
