أنس بن مالك

Anas ibn Malik

Companion and transmitter

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

Ten years of service

Anas reports that his mother and aunts urged him to serve Muhammad and that he did so for ten years in Medina.

A long transmitting life

His many narrations preserve observations of community life. This profile distinguishes what Anas says in a report from later reconstruction of his biography.

Reports differ on his exact death year and age. The account is bounded to statements in the linked Sunni hadith collection.
