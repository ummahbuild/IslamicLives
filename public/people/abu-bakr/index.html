Companionship

Abu Bakr was an early Muslim and accompanied Muhammad on the migration from Mecca to Medina in 622.

After 632

He became the first caliph after Muhammad’s death and ruled until 634. His reign included the Ridda wars and the consolidation of rule in Arabia.

Succession and religious authority are interpreted differently in Sunni and Shia traditions. A short historical summary does not settle those differences.
