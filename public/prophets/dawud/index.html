In the Qur’an

The Qur’an describes Dawud and Sulayman judging a case concerning a field.

The account continues

Both receive judgement and knowledge. The mountains and birds are described as praising Allah with Dawud.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
