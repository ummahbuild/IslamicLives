شعيب

Shuayb

Faith and honest dealing

Date not established · Madyan; exact location not asserted

In the Qur’an

Shuayb calls the people of Madyan to worship Allah and deal fairly in weights, measures, and other people’s property.

The account continues

He describes reform as his aim, within his ability, and places his trust in Allah.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
