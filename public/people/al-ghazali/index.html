Teaching and departure

Al-Ghazali taught at the Nizamiyya in Baghdad before leaving public teaching during a personal and spiritual crisis.

A wide intellectual legacy

His works address law, theology, philosophy, ethics, and spiritual discipline. The Ihya became especially influential, while his critique of philosophers generated long debate.

Qur’an and Sunnah as a shared foundation

The collection distinguishes these scholars’ historical arguments from the Qur’an and transmitted Sunnah that they interpreted. The thematic references do not certify every later opinion.

Sources give 1058 and earlier alternatives around 1055–56 for his birth. Accounts of his crisis depend partly on his own retrospective writing.
