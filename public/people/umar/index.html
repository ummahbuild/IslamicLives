عمر بن الخطاب

Umar ibn al-Khattab

The second caliph

Died 644 CE · Medina

A succession

Umar succeeded Abu Bakr in 634 and led the caliphate until 644.

A changing state

His reign saw expansion into Byzantine and Sasanian territories and changes to the administration of the growing state.

Succession and religious authority are interpreted differently in Sunni and Shia traditions. A short historical summary does not settle those differences.
