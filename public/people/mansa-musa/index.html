منسا موسى

Mansa Musa

Pilgrimage and patronage

Active in the early 14th century · Mali and the pilgrimage to Mecca

A West African ruler

Mansa Musa ruled the Mali Empire. His pilgrimage brought him through Cairo in 1324, where accounts of his wealth attracted attention.

Learning and exchange

His patronage supported Islamic buildings and scholarship. His life belongs to a wider history of trans-Saharan trade, pilgrimage, and intellectual exchange.

The end of his reign is disputed. Modern dollar valuations and “richest person ever” rankings are not reliable historical measurements.
