In the Qur’an

Yunus is named among earlier figures favoured by Allah.

The account continues

The account of the man of the fish describes a prayer from darkness, acknowledging wrongdoing; the following verse describes deliverance from distress.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
