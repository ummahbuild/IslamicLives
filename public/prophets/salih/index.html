Salih

A warning to Thamud

Date not established · Thamud; no precise location asserted

In the Qur’an

Salih calls Thamud to worship Allah and presents the she-camel as a sign. He warns them against harming her.

The account continues

The account describes the killing of the camel and the punishment that follows. Salih says he had delivered his Lord’s message and counselled his people.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: Sahih al-Bukhari preserves instructions connected with the dwellings of Thamud.
