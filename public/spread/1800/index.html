Atlas chapter · 16th–19th centuries

Across the Atlantic, under coercion

Forced migration

Muslims were among the Africans forcibly transported to the Americas through slavery. Their writings and lives preserve evidence of faith under oppression. This connection represents forced displacement.

Map markers, routes, and turquoise extents are illustrative context—not borders, ownership, exact paths, or conversion percentages.
