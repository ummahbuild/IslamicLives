لقمان

Luqman

Wisdom and counsel

Date not established · Location not established

In the source

The Qur’an describes Luqman as receiving wisdom and being told to show gratitude to Allah. His counsel to his son warns against associating partners with Allah.

His name is explicit, but these verses do not give a birth date, ethnicity, or profession. Those details are not invented.
