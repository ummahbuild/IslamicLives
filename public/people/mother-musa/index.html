Qur’anic account

An instruction and a promise

The Qur’an describes inspiration to Musa’s mother: nurse him, and when she fears for him, place him in the river without fear or grief; he will be returned and made a messenger.

Returned to his mother

Musa’s sister watches from a distance. After he refuses other nurses, his family is indicated, and he is returned to his mother so that she may be comforted.

Her personal name, age, and exact location are not given in these verses. “Inspiration” here follows the Qur’anic wording and is not a claim that she was a prophet.
