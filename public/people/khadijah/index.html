Sahabah · sourced profile

خديجة بنت خويلد

Khadijah bint Khuwaylid

Support at the beginning

Late 6th–early 7th century CE · Mecca

A return from Hira

In the report of the first revelation, Muhammad returns frightened to his wife Khadijah. She reassures him by recalling his care for relatives, guests, and people in need.

Seeking understanding

The same report says Khadijah takes Muhammad to her cousin Waraqah ibn Nawfal to hear what happened. Her response forms a central part of the transmitted account of revelation’s beginning.

Her exact birth year and age at marriage are not asserted. This account follows the Sunni hadith report.
