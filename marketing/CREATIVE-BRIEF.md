# Creative brief: IslamicLives launch

## Creative idea
**Every life opens a door to deeper learning.** Visual storytelling should signal scholarship, place, chronology and curiosity—not certainty about undocumented historical details.

## Art direction
- Contemporary editorial design inspired by manuscripts, marginal notes, annotated maps and library catalogs.
- Warm paper backgrounds, charcoal body text, subtle teal or green accent, accessible contrast.
- Use elegant geometric grid motifs sparingly; keep typography modern and readable.
- Pair a timeline rhythm (When), a schematic map shape (Where), a text card (Life), and a source reference (Story).
- Prominently label atlas imagery **Illustrative historical geography**.
- Use screenshots from verified actual builds only; never pass generated UI mockups off as product screenshots.

## Content integrity rules
Do not depict prophets, invent portraits of named historical figures, reproduce disputed border claims as fact, or claim geographic routes are exact. Avoid unverified quotations or verses in visual graphics. For Qur'anic excerpts, have a qualified reviewer check the exact text, citation and typography. Obtain consent before using a community member's name, photo, story or role credit. Confirm image/font licenses before distribution.
