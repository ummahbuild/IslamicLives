عبد الله بن عباس

Abdullah ibn Abbas

Companion and interpreter

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

A prayer for understanding

Ibn Abbas reports that Muhammad embraced him and prayed that he be taught the Book.

A transmitting Companion

His narrations became important to later Qur’anic interpretation and hadith transmission; this profile does not attribute every later tafsir report to him.

The reliability and chains of individual reports attributed to Ibn Abbas must be assessed separately.
