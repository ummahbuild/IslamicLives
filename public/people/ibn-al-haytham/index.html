ابن الهيثم

Ibn al-Haytham

Investigating light

c. 965–c. 1040 CE · Basra and Cairo

A life in inquiry

Ibn al-Haytham wrote on mathematics, astronomy, and optics. His career connected Basra with Egypt.

Light and vision

His Book of Optics examined vision, reflection, and refraction. He argued that light enters the eye from objects, and used experiments in investigating optical questions.

Accounts of his dealings with al-Hakim differ. Stories of feigned madness are not presented here as settled facts. Death dates vary around 1039–1040.
