Working in Baghdad

Al-Khwarizmi worked under the patronage of the Abbasid caliph al-Ma’mun. His mathematical writing addressed practical problems as well as methods of calculation.

Words that travelled

His algebra treatise helped give algebra its name. The word algorithm derives from the Latin form of his name; his work helped transmit Indian numeral methods.

His birthplace is uncertain and his life dates approximate. He did not invent the Indian numerals.
