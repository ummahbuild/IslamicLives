The beginning of revelation

Islamic tradition describes Muhammad receiving revelation at Hira and returning to Khadijah, who reassures him. The report is transmitted by Aisha in Sahih al-Bukhari.

A community in Medina

The migration to Medina in 622 marks a central turning point in his mission. Muhammad died in 632. The Qur’an calls him the Messenger of Allah and the seal of the prophets.

The birth year is approximate. Religious claims are identified as Qur’anic or hadith accounts.
