In the Qur’an

The Qur’an presents Ibrahim as devoted to Allah, rejecting the assignment of partners to Him.

The account continues

Ibrahim and Ismail raise the foundations of the House and ask Allah to accept their work. Their prayer also concerns the generations to come.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
