امرأة فرعون

The wife of Pharaoh

Faith under power

Date not established · Location not established

In the source

The Qur’an presents Pharaoh’s wife as an example for believers. She asks Allah for a home near Him in Paradise and for rescue from Pharaoh and wrongdoing.

Her personal name is not stated in the verse. Asiyah is a name found in hadith and later tradition and requires separate attribution.
