Islamic scholars

Learning rooted in Qur’an and Sunnah.

A bounded, sourced introduction to scholars whose work shaped Islamic law and the transmission of knowledge.

  • Abu Hanifa — Jurisprudence and legal reasoning
  • Malik ibn Anas — Hadith and the practice of Medina
  • Al-Shafi‘i — Hadith, law, and legal theory
  • Ahmad ibn Hanbal — Hadith, law, and theological witness
  • Al-Ghazali — Law, theology, ethics, and spiritual practice
  • Ibn Rushd (Averroes) — Judge, physician, and philosopher
  • Ibn Khaldun — Historian and social theorist
