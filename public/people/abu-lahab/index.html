أبو لهب

Abu Lahab

Wealth and accountability

7th century CE · Location not established

In the source

Surah al-Masad names Abu Lahab and declares that his wealth and gains will not benefit him. The chapter also refers to his wife.

This is the Qur’anic account. The chapter does not supply his full genealogy or life dates.
