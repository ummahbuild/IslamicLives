Hadith

Staying close to learn

Abu Hurayrah explains that he remained with Muhammad while others were occupied with markets or agriculture, attending and memorising what they missed.

Writing and memory

He says Abdullah ibn Amr transmitted more in writing, while he himself did not write in the same way. The report is evidence about his own account of transmission.

His birth name and exact dates are disputed in biographical literature. “Abu Hurayrah” is the well-known kunya used by the reports.
