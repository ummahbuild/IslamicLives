حفصة بنت عمر

Hafsah bint Umar

Companion and custodian of manuscripts

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

Marriage in Medina

Umar’s report describes Hafsah after the death of her husband Khunays and her subsequent marriage to Muhammad.

Manuscripts consulted

Sahih al-Bukhari reports that Qur’anic manuscripts held by Hafsah were borrowed when Uthman commissioned standard copies and then returned to her.

The exact chronology of her birth and all manuscript-history questions are outside this short profile.
