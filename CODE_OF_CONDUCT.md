# Community code of conduct

Islamic Lives welcomes careful disagreement and expects respect, honesty, patience, and humility.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence and wording without attacking people or communities.
- Distinguish correction, interpretation, and sectarian or historiographic disagreement.
- Represent sources and other contributors’ positions fairly.
- Accept that uncertainty may be the most accurate conclusion.
- Protect private information and disclose conflicts of interest.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate misquotation, doxxing, spam, and sectarian abuse are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Sensitive concerns should be reported privately through [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
