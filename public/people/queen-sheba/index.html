In the source

In the account of Sulayman, the hoopoe reports finding a woman ruling over a people, supplied with many things and possessing a great throne.

The Qur’an does not give her personal name. Bilqis belongs to later tradition; it is not substituted for the Qur’an’s unnamed identification.
