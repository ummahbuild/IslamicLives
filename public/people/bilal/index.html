بلال بن رباح

Bilal ibn Rabah

Caller to prayer and Companion

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

A practice remembered

In a report narrated by Abu Hurayrah, Muhammad asks Bilal about the deed in Islam from which he most hopes for reward. Bilal describes praying after purification.

A transmitted virtue

The report connects Bilal with footsteps heard in Paradise. It is presented as a Sunni hadith report, not an independently dated biographical event.

This concise profile does not assert disputed details about Bilal’s birth year, later residence, or death date.
