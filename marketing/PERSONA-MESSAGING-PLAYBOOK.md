# IslamicLives — Persona messaging playbook

Updated October 2026. **Public repository reveal, not a production app/store launch.** The working project describes 81 profiles and an illustrative atlas. It has no declared open-source license yet and most profiles lack documented qualified-scholar review. Always verify current status before release.

## Umbrella positioning
**Discover the lives. Understand their context. Follow the evidence.**

IslamicLives is a growing community-built educational project for exploring people in the Qur'an, Sunnah, and Islamic history, organized as **When → Where → Life → Story**.

**Brand promise:** better questions, accessible discovery, visible sources, intellectual humility.
**Community promise:** one member's initiative can become a resource improved by many.
**CTA routing:** Curious learners → explore project or a verified live experience; contributors → GitHub CONTRIBUTING.md; scholars → scoped review request; partners → bounded pilot conversation. Do not invite app download until verified.

## Persona 1 — A curious Muslim student (teens and university)
- **Tension:** “I recognize names, but I struggle to connect stories, centuries and places.”
- **Desired shift:** from isolated facts to an expandable learning journey.
- **Hook:** “A name is the beginning of the story—not the end of your learning.”
- **Value:** searchable categories, random discovery, context and references.
- **Proof:** 81 working profiles and saved discovery documented in README.
- **Objection:** “Is this a replacement for Qur'an class?” → No; educational companion requiring source checks.
- **CTA:** “Choose a life. Follow one source. Bring one question to your teacher.”
- **Short copy:** “You know the name. What about the context? IslamicLives organizes exploration around When → Where → Life → Story so you can discover a person, follow references, and keep learning.”
- **Visual:** profile-to-source journey; genuine app captures if verified.
- **Success:** source-link opens, questions submitted, profiles revisited.

## Persona 2 — A parent or caregiver
- **Tension:** “I want family learning to be engaging without relying on unsourced viral stories.”
- **Desired shift:** a shared conversation about historical people and references.
- **Hook:** “Tonight's question: where does this story come from?”
- **Value:** explore a profile together, pause at a source, distinguish certainty from uncertainty.
- **Objection:** “Is every account reviewed by scholars?” → Not yet; direct source verification and qualified teaching matter.
- **CTA:** “Make it a 10-minute family discussion—read, reference, reflect.”
- **Short copy:** “A single biography can open a family conversation about faith, learning and history. Start with a profile; follow the reference; ask what you still want to understand.”
- **Do not claim:** age suitability ratings, child safety assessments, parental controls or child-focused production readiness.

## Persona 3 — Islamic school / madrasah educator
- **Tension:** “Teaching context and source literacy takes preparation.”
- **Desired shift:** from presentation-only storytelling to student-directed evidence checks.
- **Hook:** “Not just who. Ask when, where, how we know.”
- **Value:** lesson starters, source distinctions, illustrative maps.
- **Objection:** “Is this authoritative curriculum?” → No; proposed classroom aid subject to educator review and source validation.
- **CTA:** “Review a bounded teaching exercise and tell us what needs improving.”
- **Short copy:** “IslamicLives invites students to explore historical figures with four questions: When? Where? Life? Story? Its evidence labels and uncertainty notes can become prompts for research literacy—not shortcuts around teachers.”
- **Pilot:** educator tests one profile and reports citation clarity, readability, misconceptions.
- **Success:** qualified educator feedback and documented corrections.

## Persona 4 — History enthusiast / lifelong learner
- **Tension:** “I want historical breadth without having to assume every chronology is exact.”
- **Desired shift:** connected exploration with explicit uncertainty.
- **Hook:** “Travel through history without treating the map as certainty.”
- **Value:** atlas chapters, diverse biographies and cross-context reading.
- **Objection:** “Do atlas routes mark exactly where people traveled?” → No; illustrative extents, not exact paths or borders.
- **CTA:** “Explore a period. Inspect a reference. Add a research question.”
- **Copy:** “From biographies to an illustrative atlas, IslamicLives invites you to navigate the people and geography of Islamic history—and notice where the surviving evidence leaves room for debate.”

## Persona 5 — Revert / new Muslim
- **Tension:** “There are unfamiliar names, spellings and time periods; I don't know where to begin.”
- **Desired shift:** a respectful, non-intimidating doorway into further learning.
- **Hook:** “You don't need to know every name to begin.”
- **Value:** approachable browsing, categories and source-linked context.
- **Objection:** “Will it tell me what to believe or do?” → It is not fatwa or religious guidance service.
- **CTA:** “Start with one name and bring your questions to a trusted teacher.”
- **Copy:** “New to the people and periods discussed in Islamic learning? Start small. Discover one profile, see the references, and take your next question into a class or study circle.”
- **Guardrail:** avoid framing new Muslims as less capable or implying coverage includes a comprehensive beginner curriculum.

## Persona 6 — Arabic-language reader / translator
- **Tension:** “Transliterations, titles and source editions can distort meaning.”
- **Desired shift:** precise terminology with transparent variant forms.
- **Hook:** “Names and translations are research decisions.”
- **Value:** opportunity to improve wording and source context.
- **Objection:** “Are all Arabic editions validated?” → No such claim.
- **CTA:** “Suggest one precise, evidenced terminology or citation improvement.”
- **Copy:** “Good translation is more than swapping words. Help IslamicLives distinguish titles, naming variants and source editions while preserving the meaning and uncertainty of each record.”

## Persona 7 — Islamic scholar / qualified reviewer
- **Tension:** “Digital biographies often flatten source hierarchies and overclaim authority.”
- **Desired shift:** scoped, attributable review with clear evidence trails.
- **Hook:** “We don't need a blanket endorsement. We need precise review.”
- **Value:** source separation, correction record, limited review scope.
- **Objection:** “Will my involvement be called certification?” → No; name, method and scope require consent and written record.
- **CTA:** “Review one bounded profile or claim set under an agreed methodology.”
- **Copy:** “IslamicLives is seeking qualified reviewers to examine specified claims, source alignment and uncertainty—not to rubber-stamp a database. The repository explicitly recognizes that most profiles have not undergone documented scholarly review.”
- **Success:** accepted scoped review, corrected claim records, documented reviewer scope.

## Persona 8 — Academic historian / digital humanist
- **Tension:** “Popular digital history collapses competing chronologies and treats schematic maps as exact.”
- **Desired shift:** transparent provenance and reusable methodology.
- **Hook:** “A map is an argument. Label its uncertainty.”
- **Value:** explicit historical caveats, primary and academic source categories, research audit opportunities.
- **CTA:** “Help review a chronology or atlas chapter with identifiable sources.”
- **Copy:** “IslamicLives is experimenting with navigable biography and historically cautious visualization. The atlas uses illustrative geography, and research notes distinguish known claims from disputed reconstruction. We welcome critiques that make those distinctions clearer.”
- **Guardrail:** don't assert academic institutional partnership.

## Persona 9 — Full-stack / web / Expo developer
- **Tension:** “I want useful work with a real codebase and explicit issue boundaries.”
- **Desired shift:** meaningful PRs on accessibility, testing and performance.
- **Hook:** “Make historical learning easier to access—with one good PR.”
- **Value:** web + Expo workspace, generated data, audits, Three.js atlas.
- **Objection:** “Is it open source?” → Public repo, but no license declared; clarify legal permissions before reuse.
- **CTA:** “Read CONTRIBUTING.md; pick a bounded issue; run npm run ci.”
- **Copy:** “Interested in education technology, Three.js, Expo or web accessibility? IslamicLives is a public GitHub project with source-linked data and interactive exploration. Improve the experience with focused, reviewed contributions.”
- **Success:** reproducible issues, PRs accepted, accessibility fixes, testing coverage.

## Persona 10 — Designer / accessibility specialist
- **Tension:** “Interactive history can exclude people with reduced vision, motion sensitivity or low-end devices.”
- **Desired shift:** inclusive navigation beyond the 3D demo.
- **Hook:** “History should be navigable without a perfect device.”
- **Value:** readable profile fallback, keyboard, motion, screen-reader and small-viewport improvements.
- **CTA:** “Audit a journey for keyboard, assistive tech and mobile readability.”
- **Copy:** “A 3D atlas is compelling—but learning must remain accessible without it. Help IslamicLives make reading, browsing and source exploration work for more people.”
- **Guardrail:** don't claim completed native device or accessibility certification.

## Persona 11 — Muslim founder / builder / community organizer
- **Tension:** “Great conversations rarely become maintained community projects.”
- **Desired shift:** a replicable culture of initiative and stewardship.
- **Hook:** “A community is what its members build together.”
- **Value:** member-initiated story, public documentation, opportunities for shared work.
- **CTA:** “Share a focused improvement, introduce a collaborator, or propose a community review session.”
- **Copy:** “One member of the ummah.build community helped initiate IslamicLives. The bigger idea is that builders can turn their expertise into tools others learn from—and invite the next person to make them better.”

## Persona 12 — Institution / library / nonprofit / sponsor
- **Tension:** “We want educational impact but cannot endorse unreviewed claims.”
- **Desired shift:** low-risk, measurable pilot with appropriate review.
- **Hook:** “Start with a small learning experiment, not a blanket endorsement.”
- **Value:** specific feedback pilots, source-alignment review, access and translation opportunities.
- **CTA:** “Discuss a bounded research or educator pilot.”
- **Copy:** “IslamicLives is looking for partners willing to evaluate specific educational experiences and help improve evidence quality and accessibility. Production status, scholarly review and licensing limitations are disclosed upfront.”
- **Success:** documented pilot findings and contributor referrals, never implied affiliation.

## Messaging by funnel stage

|---|---|---|
| Awareness | A community member helped start a more connected way to explore Islamic history | See GitHub repository |
| Interest | When → Where → Life → Story, 81 profiles and illustrative atlas | Read product overview |

| Consideration | Clear limits and an auditable research process | Read methodology and contributor guidelines |
| Participation | One bounded improvement makes the project more valuable | Open an issue or PR |
| Advocacy | Celebrate corrected sources and accessible learning experiences | Share a verified milestone |
