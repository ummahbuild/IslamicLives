# Product messaging and learning value

## North star
**Discover the lives. Trace the context. Follow the evidence.**

IslamicLives is a growing, source-aware educational experience for exploring people in the Qur'an, Sunnah and Islamic history—organized around **When → Where → Life → Story**.

## One-line descriptions
- Educational: Explore Islamic history through biographies, references and historical context.
- Technical: A searchable, content-driven web/Expo project with a Three.js historical atlas.
- Community: A growing public project shaped by careful community contributions.
- Responsible research: Explore named lives with explicit sourcing and visible uncertainty.

## Audiences and jobs-to-be-done
| Audience | Need | Message | Responsible action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students | Orient to people and eras | Start with a life; follow the references | Compare with taught primary texts |
| Families | Explore together | Find a figure, read, and discuss | Seek qualified educators for interpretation |
| Teachers | Organize research prompts | Use categories and citations to begin | Independently check each source |
| Curious readers | Connect time, place and story | Discover a related era or person | Treat atlas geometry as illustrative |
| Researchers and scholars | Correct or qualify | Evidence types and review status are visible | Submit bounded source improvements |
| Engineers/designers | Improve access | Shared web/Expo content and auditable workflow | Follow CONTRIBUTING.md |

## Repository-documented features (not certification)
1. 81 profiles (current working edition), including 25 prophets from the commonly taught list.
2. Biographies across additional Qur'anic figures, Companions, scholars, scientists, travelers, rulers and builders.
3. Source-linked entries and visible uncertainty.
4. Qur'anic passages and separately labeled hadith reports.
5. Search and category filtering.
6. Random discovery.
7. Saved Lives.
8. Shareable profile URLs.
9. Three.js atlas with sourced chapters.
10. Illustrative geographic extents, camera tours and 0.5x/1x/2x playback.
11. 16 atlas chapters documented in CHANGELOG.
12. Crawlable pages, metadata, sitemap, robots.txt and llms.txt.
13. Expo SDK 57 codebase sharing generated content with the web application.
14. Repository editorial workflow and structural validation harness.

## What learners can practice
- Situate a historical person in a broader narrative.
- Distinguish Qur'anic scripture, transmitted hadith, later historical narration, and modern research.
- Find source references rather than relying on an unsupported summary.
- Recognize variant chronologies, uncertain identities and illustrative maps.
- Build questions for further reading and qualified discussion.

## FAQ
**What is IslamicLives?** A source-aware biographical exploration project for named people from the Qur'an, Sunnah and Islamic history.
**Who is included?** The working collection currently has 81 profiles across prophets and other historical categories; coverage is growing.
**Is it a fatwa or authoritative religious reference?** No. It is an educational entry point; claims require source checking and qualified review.
**Is every profile verified by an Islamic scholar?** No. Most profiles lack documented qualified-scholar review.
**Are atlas paths exact?** No. Routes and extents are illustrative, not exact historical journeys or borders.
**Is it open source?** The repository is public and accepts contributions, but no open-source license has yet been specified.
**Can I download the app?** An Expo app is in the repository. Public App Store/Play Store availability is not verified and native release testing remains outstanding.
**How can I help?** Follow the existing contribution process and submit research corrections, accessible design changes, testing or code.
