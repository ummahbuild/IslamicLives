مريم

Maryam

Devotion and the birth of Isa

Date not established · Location not established

Chosen and purified

The Qur’an describes angels announcing to Maryam that Allah has chosen and purified her.

The announcement

Surah Maryam recounts her withdrawal from her family and the arrival of a messenger announcing a son. Her question about bearing a child is part of the account.

No exact date or birthplace is supplied by these passages. Maryam’s status as a prophet is debated; she is classified here as a Qur’anic person.
