Prophets · sourced profile

نوح

Nuh

A messenger to his people

Date not established · Location not established

In the Qur’an

Nuh calls his people to worship Allah alone. The Qur’an describes opposition from their leaders.

The account continues

He is instructed to construct the ark. Later, the account names al-Judi as its resting place; a precise modern identification is not asserted here.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: The intercession report names Nuh and describes his mission to his people.
