Abu Talha al-Ansari

Companion of Medina

Date

Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

A garden given in charity

After the verse about attaining righteousness through giving what one loves, Abu Talha offers his garden Bayruha in charity.

Direction for the gift

The report says Muhammad commends the gift and advises that it be distributed among Abu Talha’s relatives.

His personal name and dates appear with variants in later biographical sources; this account stays with the linked report.
