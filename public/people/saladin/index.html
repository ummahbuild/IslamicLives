Power and alliances

Salah al-Din, known as Saladin, built his power in Egypt and Syria through both military action and diplomacy.

Jerusalem in 1187

His forces defeated the Crusader army at Hattin and captured Jerusalem in 1187. His career also involved wars against Muslim rivals; it cannot be reduced to later stories of chivalry.

Birth dates are given as 1137 or 1138 in different references. Later heroic literature is not treated as direct evidence.
