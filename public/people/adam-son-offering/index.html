Qur’anic account

Two offerings

The Qur’an asks that the account of Adam’s two sons be recited truthfully: each offers a sacrifice, and one is accepted while the other is not.

Moral responsibility

The brothers’ exchange rejects aggression; the account then describes one soul prompting the killing of the other and the killer becoming one of the losers.

The Qur’an does not give either son a personal name. The names Habil and Qabil belong to later interpretive tradition and are not used here as if they appeared in the verse.
