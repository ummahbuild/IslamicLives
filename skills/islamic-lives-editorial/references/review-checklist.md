# Profile review checklist

- Identity and transliteration are consistent.
- Each Qur’anic claim has an exact verse range.
- Name-token indexes are not described as complete narrative coverage.
- Each hadith link resolves to the cited collection and number.
- Hadith grading and tradition are identified where relevant.
- Dates and places are either supported or explicitly unestablished.
- Later exegesis is attributed and not presented as Qur’anic wording.
- Sunni, Shia, and legal-school differences are attributed without ranking unless the page has a declared scope.
- The uncertainty note names the real limits of the evidence.
- The generated HTML contains the same claim and sources as the interactive view.
- A qualified human review is still required for a `scholar-reviewed` status.
