A named transmitter

Umm Salamah transmits reports concerning worship, family life, and events in the early Muslim community.

Identity in the sources

The narrator catalogue identifies her as Hind bint Abi Umayyah and a wife of Muhammad; this profile retains her well-known kunya.

Reports differ on her death year. This is an introductory account, not an exhaustive hadith or biographical index.
