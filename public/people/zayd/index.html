Sahabah · sourced profile

زيد بن حارثة

Zayd ibn Harithah

A named Companion

7th century CE · Location not established

In the source

The Qur’an explicitly names Zayd in the passage concerning the end of his marriage and the Prophet’s subsequent marriage.

The verse itself names Zayd; the full identification is supplied by the tafsir shown with the verse. The Qur’an does not name his former wife in this passage.
