Qur’anic account

A report from two women

After Musa helps two women water their flock, one returns and says her father invites him so he may be rewarded. Musa tells the elder his story and is reassured.

An agreed term

The elder offers Musa marriage to one of his daughters in return for eight years of service, with ten left as Musa’s choice. Musa accepts the agreement.

The Qur’an does not name this man or identify him as the prophet Shuayb. That common later identification is therefore not stated as Qur’anic fact.
