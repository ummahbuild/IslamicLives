عثمان بن عفان

Uthman ibn Affan

The third caliph

Died 656 CE · Medina

Leadership

Uthman succeeded Umar and ruled from 644 to 656. His death came during a rebellion against his government.

The written Qur’an

Sahih al-Bukhari reports Uthman commissioning copies of the Qur’an from manuscripts held by Hafsah and sending copies to different regions.

Succession and religious authority are interpreted differently in Sunni and Shia traditions. A short historical summary does not settle those differences.
