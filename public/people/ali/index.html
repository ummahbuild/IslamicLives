Family and community

Ali was Muhammad’s cousin and son-in-law. He became caliph in 656.

A divided community

His caliphate unfolded during the first civil war. He was assassinated in 661. Sunni tradition counts him as the fourth rightly guided caliph; Shia tradition recognises him as the first imam.

Succession and religious authority are interpreted differently in Sunni and Shia traditions. A short historical summary does not settle those differences.
