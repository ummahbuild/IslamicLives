Dhul-Kifl

Patience in a brief account

Date not established · Location not established

In the Qur’an

Dhul-Kifl is named with Ismail and Idris among the patient.

The account continues

The following verse includes them among the righteous admitted to divine mercy. The text does not give Dhul-Kifl a detailed life story.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted. Commonly included in the traditional list of 25 prophets; commentators differ over whether these verses establish his prophethood. Identification with Ezekiel is not asserted.
