Atlas chapter · 661–750 CE

From the Atlantic to the Indus

Political expansion

The Umayyad caliphate, centred in Damascus, extended across North Africa and into Iberia and the Indus region. The markers locate selected parts of this realm; they do not measure conversion.

Map markers, routes, and turquoise extents are illustrative context—not borders, ownership, exact paths, or conversion percentages.
