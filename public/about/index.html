Sources and approach

Named people. Traceable accounts. Honest limits.

Qur’anic accounts, hadith reports, historical primary texts, and modern scholarship are labelled separately. Unsupported dates, locations, dialogue, and appearance are not invented.

Coverage

  • Traditionally listed Qur’anic prophets: 25 of 25 entries present; every profile has direct claim citations and a curated key-passage index. Narrative, pronoun, tafsir, and hadith corpus coverage is still not exhaustive.
  • Qur’anic name-reference index: 38 people; 649 source-tagged word positions. Source concept queries reconciled; pronouns, alternate names and source omissions remain outside this count.
  • Other people in the Qur’an: In progress; named and unnamed individuals require a complete verse audit
  • Sahabah and hadith narrators: Partial; collection and edition boundaries awaiting definition
  • Islamic scholars: 4 introductory jurist profiles with separate biographical, Qur’anic, and hadith references; not a complete scholar index
  • Historical works after 632: Partial; a bounded bibliography and person index are required
  • Spread of Islam through 2026: 16 interactive atlas chapters from scriptural beginnings to 2026; six sourced global population snapshots and six regional locators for 2010/2020. Selected historical connections, not exhaustive geographic coverage.
