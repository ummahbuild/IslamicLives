Dhul-Qarnayn

A ruler tested with power

Qur’anic people

Qur’anic account

A means to travel

The Qur’an says Dhul-Qarnayn was established in the land and given means to pursue a course. The account then describes journeys without supplying a calendar date.

A barrier by consent

Between two barriers, a people ask for protection from Gog and Magog. Dhul-Qarnayn refuses tribute, asks for their labour, and constructs a barrier while attributing its success to divine mercy.

The Qur’an does not identify Dhul-Qarnayn with Alexander, Cyrus, or another historical ruler. The locations and chronology are not asserted here.
