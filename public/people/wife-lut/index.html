امرأة لوط

The wife of Lut

Responsibility beyond family ties

Date not established · Location not established

In the source

The wife of Lut appears with the wife of Nuh in the Qur’an’s example of people whose family relationships did not protect them from judgement.

The verse does not give her personal name. Her entry remains distinct from the wife of Nuh.
