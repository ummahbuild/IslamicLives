## LinkedIn 02 — engineering and ethics
**Can we treat historical uncertainty as a product requirement?**

When developing educational software, the interface can make an unsupported claim look surprisingly authoritative.

IslamicLives is an attempt to take the opposite direction. Its biographies use source links and separate evidence types. Its historical atlas uses illustrative extents rather than claiming precise routes. The repository includes editorial checks, validation scripts and documented research boundaries.

There is still work to do, especially qualified scholarly review, accessibility and native-device testing. We see that transparency as part of building responsibly, not as a weakness.

The project arose through the initiative of an ummah.build community member. Engineers, historians, educators and reviewers can explore the public repository and contribute with care:
https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives

## Email launch — long form
**Subject:** Introducing IslamicLives — a new project from our community
**Preview:** Discover source-linked lives and an illustrative atlas—and help shape what's next.

Assalamu alaikum,

We're excited to share **IslamicLives**, an educational project initiated with the help of a member of the ummah.build community.

The idea is simple: what if learning about people in Islamic history felt less like memorizing isolated names and more like discovering lives in context?

IslamicLives organizes exploration around **When → Where → Life → Story**. The current working edition features 81 profiles, search, Saved Lives, source links and an interactive historical atlas. Readers can explore people from the Qur'an, Sunnah and Islamic history while seeing which details are sourced and which remain uncertain.

We are intentionally keeping the project's limitations visible. The collection is not exhaustive; atlas routes are illustrative; and further qualified scholarly review and production testing remain priorities.

The GitHub repository is public, and we'd love your thoughtful feedback. Whether you're an educator, researcher, developer, designer or accessibility tester, there are meaningful ways to help.

**Explore the repository:** https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives
**How to contribute:** https://github.com/ummahbuild/IslamicLives/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

May Allah bless everyone contributing to beneficial knowledge.

The ummah.build community
