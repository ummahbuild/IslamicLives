A new timescale

Ahmed Zewail’s research made it possible to study atomic motion during chemical reactions on femtosecond timescales. This work helped establish femtochemistry.

Science in a connected world

A Caltech professor, he received the 1999 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. His later work included four-dimensional electron microscopy. He died in 2016.

Included in the modern history of people from the Muslim world; scientific achievement is not used as evidence for a theological claim.
