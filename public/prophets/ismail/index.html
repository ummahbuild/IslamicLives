إسماعيل

Ismail

A promise kept

Date not established · The House in the Qur’anic account

In the Qur’an

Ismail is described as a messenger and prophet who keeps his word and instructs his people in prayer and charity.

The account continues

He joins Ibrahim in raising the foundations of the House, praying that their work will be accepted.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: Sahih al-Bukhari transmits an account involving Ibrahim, Ismail, and the sanctuary.
