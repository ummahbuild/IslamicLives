فاطمة بنت محمد

Fatimah bint Muhammad

Daughter of Muhammad

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

A daughter remembered

Sahih al-Bukhari preserves Muhammad’s statement that Fatimah is part of him and that what angers her angers him.

Work and remembrance

Another report describes Fatimah’s work at the hand mill and the remembrance taught to her and Ali before sleep.

Reports about her age, birth year, and the political disputes after Muhammad’s death require wider Sunni and Shia source review; no exact birth year is asserted.
