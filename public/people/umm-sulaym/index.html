Umm Sulaym

Companion and mother of Anas

Date
7th century CE
Associated place
Medina
Collection
Sahabah
Source types
Hadith

A request for Anas

Anas narrates that Umm Sulaym asked Muhammad to pray for her son. The report explicitly identifies Anas as his servant.

A household in the reports

Her life appears through reports transmitted by and about her family; those reports do not supply a single complete chronological biography.

Her personal-name variants and the chronology of her marriages require broader biographical-source comparison.
