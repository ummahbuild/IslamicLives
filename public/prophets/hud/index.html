The call to ‘Ad

Date not established · The people of ‘Ad; exact location not established

In the Qur’an

Hud is sent to ‘Ad and calls them to worship Allah. He asks no payment from his people for delivering the message.

The account continues

The Qur’an describes Hud and those who believe with him being rescued by divine mercy.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
