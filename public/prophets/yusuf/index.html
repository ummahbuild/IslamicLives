In the Qur’an

Yusuf asks to be entrusted with the storehouses of the land, describing his ability to protect and manage them.

The account continues

When his brothers face him, he releases them from reproach and asks that Allah forgive them.

A place named in the account

Yusuf welcomes his parents to Egypt in the Qur’anic account. The region is named even though the verse does not establish a birth date or exact city.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
