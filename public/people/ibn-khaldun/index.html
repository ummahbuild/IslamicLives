Politics and scholarship

Born in Tunis, Ibn Khaldun served several North African courts before periods of withdrawal, travel, and teaching.

The Muqaddimah

The introduction to his universal history examines social solidarity, political authority, economic life, and the rise and decline of dynasties. Later labels such as “founder of sociology” can obscure its medieval intellectual setting.

Qur’an and Sunnah as a shared foundation

The collection distinguishes these scholars’ historical arguments from the Qur’an and transmitted Sunnah that they interpreted. The thematic references do not certify every later opinion.

His autobiography is a major source for his life but remains a self-authored account. Modern disciplinary titles are not treated as exact descriptions of his project.
