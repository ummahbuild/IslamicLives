يحيى

Yahya

The gift of a son

Date not established · Location not established

In the Qur’an

Yahya is the name given in the announcement to Zakariyya that he will have a son.

The account continues

He is also named among the righteous in Surah al-An‘am’s list of earlier prophetic figures.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: The Night Journey report names Yahya among the prophets Muhammad meets.
