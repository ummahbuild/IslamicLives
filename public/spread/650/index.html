Atlas chapter · 632–661 CE

Beyond the Arabian Peninsula

Political expansion

Under the early caliphs, Arab armies took Syria, Egypt, Iraq and Iran. Political control expanded rapidly; the populations of these lands did not all become Muslim at once.

Map markers, routes, and turquoise extents are illustrative context—not borders, ownership, exact paths, or conversion percentages.
