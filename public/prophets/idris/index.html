إدريس

Idris

Truthfulness and patience

Date not established · Location not established

In the Qur’an

Idris is described as a truthful prophet, raised to a high station.

The account continues

He is named with Ismail and Dhul-Kifl among those who were patient. These brief passages do not supply a full biography.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.

Sunnah reference

Referenced: The Night Journey report names Idris among the prophets Muhammad meets.
