البيروني

Al-Biruni

Astronomy across cultures

973–c. 1048 CE · Khwarazm and Ghazna

Observation and calculation

Born near Kath in Khwarazm, al-Biruni studied astronomy and mathematics. His observations helped determine geographical positions.

Studying India

He studied Sanskrit sources and wrote about Indian religion, customs, astronomy, and learning. His work emerged amid political upheaval and Ghaznavid expansion.

Dates of particular journeys are uncertain. This summary does not claim his travel was voluntary.
