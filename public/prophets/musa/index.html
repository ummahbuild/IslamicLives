Prophets · sourced profile

موسى

Musa

A messenger and a brother

Date not established · Egypt; later Madyan

In the Qur’an

Musa asks for his brother Harun to assist him and share in his task.

The account continues

Surah Maryam describes Musa as a chosen messenger and prophet, called from the side of Mount Tur.

A place named in the account

The Qur’an explicitly locates Musa and his brother’s community in Egypt. This identifies a region, not a precise house or birthplace.

The Qur’an does not give a calendar date for this life. This is a scriptural account; no independently verified date or precise geographical location is asserted.
