# IslamicLives — earned media and partnership playbook

## Objectives
1. Celebrate the originating community member appropriately.
2. Reach learners and educators through trustworthy partners.
3. Recruit specific source-alignment and qualified scholarly reviewers.
4. Attract engineers and accessibility contributors.
5. Grow awareness without overclaiming a released or scholar-certified reference.

## Target partner categories
- Islamic education nonprofits and schools: feedback on learner pathways, not blanket endorsement.
- University Islamic studies and history departments: bounded bibliographic review.
- Islamic libraries and manuscript projects: legitimate source discovery and licensing.
- Arabic language teachers and translation specialists: careful naming and terminology.
- Accessibility advocates: usability on lower-end devices and assistive technologies.
- Muslim developer communities: mentorship, code reviews and testing.
- Public history and digital humanities practitioners: cartographic caveats and editorial architecture.

## Partnership packages
- **Research review sprint:** 3–5 bounded claims with precise sources and proposed changes.
- **Accessibility clinic:** 30-minute observed assistive-technology test with reproducible reports.
- **Educator pilot:** guided 15-minute reading exercise, followed by qualitative feedback.
- **Developer walkthrough:** architecture review of generated content and atlas fallback.
- **Community showcase:** approved origin story and demo with explicit development status.

## Evaluation
Track meaningful edits, qualified reviewer referrals, documented user feedback and merged accessibility fixes—not merely impressions.

## Policy
Do not claim school or scholar endorsements. Secure consent for testimonials and interviews. Do not send bulk unsolicited messages or create deceptive partner affiliations.
