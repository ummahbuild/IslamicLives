أخت موسى

The sister of Musa

Watching from a distance

Date not established · Location not established

In the source

Musa’s mother tells his sister to follow him. The account describes her watching him from a distance without the others noticing.

The Qur’an does not state her personal name. This entry does not import a name from another tradition.
